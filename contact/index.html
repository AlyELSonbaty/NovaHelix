Why we exist

Good research questions are common. Finding the right people to work on them is not. Nova Helix closes that gap.

A

Leads come from anywhere

A research lead can be a question, a dataset, or an early hypothesis — submitted by anyone with something worth investigating.

B

Researchers choose their orbit

Researchers browse open leads in their field and join the ones they're equipped to move forward, regardless of institution or geography.

C

Fully remote, by design

No office, no home country — just the collaboration. It's how we work across 25+ countries without asking anyone to relocate.

How it works

Tell the research lead why you're a great fit

The lead reads every application, and specific details make it much easier to say yes. Try to cover these four points, focusing on what's relevant to this paper's topic:

  • 1
    Your research experience — projects, lab or clinical work, thesis, relevant courses.e.g. "Final-year project on glucose metabolism in cell lines, 6 months in the biochemistry lab."
  • 2
    Publications, posters or talks — title, where it was published or presented (or "in preparation"), and your role.e.g. "Co-author, 'Title of paper', Journal X, 2025 — I did the literature search."
  • 3
    What exactly you worked on — your part in it: methods, tools or software, data you handled, what you wrote or analysed.e.g. "Screened 400 abstracts, extracted data, and ran the meta-analysis in R."
  • 4
    What you'd bring to this paper — your skills and how many hours a week you can give.
Write at least a few sentences covering the points above.0 / 3000

Pick your country code, then type your number without the code or a leading 0. A number that works on WhatsApp is ideal. Only this paper's research lead and the Nova Helix team can see it.

By submitting, you agree that your application and phone number are shared with this paper's research lead and the Nova Helix team. See our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, and the choices you have. Plain language, no tricks.

Last updated: October 26, 2026. Nova Helix Research is an independent, remote-first research collective. We keep our data practices simple and only ask for what the platform needs to work.

1. Who we are

"Nova Helix Research", "we", "us" and "our" means the independent collective that runs this website and the research collaboration platform on it. You can reach us at any time at novahelix.contact@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhenWhat we collect
You create an accountFirst and last name, email address, password (stored securely hashed by our authentication provider, never in plain text), country.
You fill in your profileField of research, short bio, LinkedIn link, profile photo. All optional.
You apply to join a paperThe application text you write (experience, publications, skills, availability) and your phone number.
You take a courseEnrollment, lesson progress, quiz interaction, certificates issued to you.
You pay for a coursePayment is handled by Stripe on its own checkout page. We never see or store your card details; we only record that a payment was made.
You contact us or fill in a Google FormWhatever you choose to send us (for example when proposing a research lead or asking to join as a researcher).
You simply browseBasic technical data your browser sends (such as IP address and device type) is processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and let you log in.
  • To list researchers, research leads and board members on the public pages.
  • To pass your paper applications to the research lead of that paper, and to email you when you are accepted.
  • To run courses, track progress and issue certificates.
  • To email you about things you asked for or that directly concern your account or applications. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To keep the platform secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. What is public and what is private

Public: if you are listed as a researcher or research lead (or are a board member), your name, photo, country, field, bio, LinkedIn link and the papers you work on appear on the website for anyone to see. You control most of these details from your profile page and can leave optional ones empty.

Private: your email address, phone number, application text, course progress and payment status are not shown publicly. Your application text and phone number are visible only to the research lead of the paper you applied to and to the Nova Helix administrators.

5. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to operate the platform. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (profile photos, paper files, certificates).
  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • Google — Google Forms (proposal and researcher sign-up forms), Gmail (sending notification emails) and Google Fonts (page fonts).
  • Stripe — payment processing for paid courses.
  • jsDelivr — delivers a code library the site needs to run.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our members.

6. International transfers

Our members work from many countries and our providers operate globally, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where required, we rely on the safeguards offered by our providers, such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and profile for as long as your account exists. Application messages are kept while the related paper is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile and personal data, except where we are required to keep something by law (for example payment records held by Stripe).

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it — you can edit most of it yourself on your profile page;
  • delete it, or restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us at novahelix.contact@gmail.com. We will answer within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, passwords are hashed, and access to private information is limited by account role. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a login session (in local storage) so you stay signed in; that is strictly necessary for the platform to work. Third-party services you use through us, such as Stripe checkout or Google Forms, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

11. Children

The platform is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let account holders know. Continued use of the platform after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: novahelix.contact@gmail.com, or use the Contact page.
